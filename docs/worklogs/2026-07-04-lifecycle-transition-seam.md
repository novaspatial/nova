# 2026-07-04 — Lifecycle transition seam (#34)

I picked up the top item in the Now queue: a live bug where the portal had no rules about which project stage can move to which. A client could pull an already-delivered project back to an earlier stage (and trigger a bogus "we received your files" email), and on the studio side any status jump was accepted. Digging in before building, I found it was worse than the ticket said: a client could skip the site entirely, talk to the database directly, and set their project to any stage they liked — including making an unpaid project look paid so they could upload files for free.

I brought 4 decisions to Onur before writing code: go ahead as scoped, add a lock at the database level too, remove a button that let the studio "send a mix for review" at a stage where no mix can exist yet, and keep a studio-only shortcut for advancing a stalled client. He said yes to all 4, so the shape was settled up front.

The fix is 1 central rulebook that says who may move a project from where to where. Every page, button, and email that used to keep its own private list of stages now reads that rulebook — 5 conflicting copies deleted. The database itself got a matching lock, so even going around the site a client can only do the 1 thing they're supposed to: hand their finished upload over for review. The 2 extra problems I found along the way (faking a paid project at creation time, and the payment-check fallback that has been quietly broken since July 2) were too big to smuggle into this ticket, so I filed them as #40 and #41.

Then I proved it works: 580 automated tests pass, and I rehearsed the real thing — created a throwaway account, placed a 0-dollar test order, walked it through the flow, tried the old cheats and watched them bounce, then deleted every trace. Committed, pushed, CI green, ticket closed. The next 2 features in line (order emails and the file purge) can now read the rulebook instead of inventing their own lists.
